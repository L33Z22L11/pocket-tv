1. Install this firmware from the play page and download the matching package from the source repository’s v1.1.0 Release. Install Python 3.10–3.13 on your computer, connect the device with a USB data cable, and run tv.sh (macOS/Linux) or tv.cmd (Windows). The first launch needs internet access to install dependencies.
2. Search for a channel, select it, then press Enter or click “换台” (Switch). Volume initially starts at zero. In landscape, tap ↑ to lower volume and ↓ to raise it; portrait reverses these volume controls. Hold ↑ for the previous channel or ↓ for the next. Tap OK to pause/resume; hold OK to toggle help.
3. USB playback needs no Wi-Fi setup. For Wi-Fi, keep USB connected and press W on the computer. Choose a scanned 2.4 GHz network, enter its password, and click “连接并保存” (Connect and save); use “手动输入” (Manual entry) for a hidden network. After “Wi-Fi 已连接” and an address appear, select a channel again. Correct the password or rescan to retry; use the same entry to change networks. Computer and device must be reachable on the same LAN; captive portals and enterprise authentication are unsupported.
4. Keep the computer player running. Disconnecting USB attempts paired Wi-Fi; reconnecting USB switches back. The device still needs power, and handover requires buffering. Press Ctrl+Q to stop and exit.
